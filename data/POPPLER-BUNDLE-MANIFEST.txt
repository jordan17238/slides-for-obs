Intentionally omitted from the original release:
  - 10 other poppler tools (pdftotext, pdftocairo, pdfimages, ...)
    -> not used by this plugin; dead weight
  - share/poppler (13 MB of CJK encoding data)
    -> only needed for PDFs with NON-embedded CJK fonts; LibreOffice
       embeds fonts in its PDF exports. If CJK slide text ever
       renders blank on Windows, restore it as data/share/poppler.
  - Library/share (man pages) -> no runtime value
